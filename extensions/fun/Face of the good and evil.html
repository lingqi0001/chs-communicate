Frequently Asked Questions

How your photos are processed and kept private.

Is my photo uploaded or saved anywhere?

No. Everything runs entirely in your browser using local canvas memory. Your photos never leave your device, and no data is ever sent to a server.

How does Auto Align work?

It uses browser face detection to locate key landmarks like eyes and nose bridge, then aligns the symmetry axis automatically. You can still adjust the handles manually anytime.

Why do the left and right mirrors look like different people?

Almost nobody has a perfectly symmetrical face. Mirroring each side creates two distinct composite faces, highlighting natural variations in expression, lighting, and structure.
